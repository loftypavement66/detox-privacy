Detox Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short: Detox doesn't collect, send or share any data. Everything it stores stays on your iPhone.

What Detox stores

To work, Detox keeps a few settings on your device only:

  • The apps and websites you choose to limit. Apple gives Detox these as anonymous tokens, so Detox can't see the names of your apps or websites.
  • Your daily time limit.
  • Whether hardcore mode is on, and when it started and ends.
  • The time today's block started.

Screen Time data

Detox uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to block apps once your daily limit is used up. Apple runs these on your device and doesn't give Detox your app usage history. Detox never receives, stores or sends information about how you use other apps.

What Detox doesn't do

  • It doesn't connect to the internet.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It contains no third-party code or services.
  • It doesn't share or sell any data, because it has none to share.

Deleting your data

Deleting Detox removes everything it stored. While hardcore mode is on, iOS prevents the app from being deleted until the lock ends. You can also remove Detox's Screen Time access at any time in Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access.

Children

Detox is for limiting your own app use. It doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
